This notice applies to the HealthOS marketing website only — the forms on this site and the analytics behind them. It does not cover the HealthOS clinical platform used inside a hospital, which is governed by the agreement with that hospital.

Legal basis

We rely on your consent, given by ticking the box on the form. You may withdraw it at any time, and we will delete what we hold unless we are required to keep it. Withdrawing does not affect anything done before you withdrew.

How long we keep it

  • Analytics — visit and page-event records are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Enquiries and demo requests — kept while we are in contact and for a reasonable period afterwards, because they are business records. Ask us and we will delete yours.

Who else sees it

  • Supabase — stores the database.
  • Render — runs the website.
  • Resend — sends the notification and acknowledgement emails.

These providers process data on our instructions. We do not sell your details or use them for advertising.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023 you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you;
  • correct anything wrong;
  • delete it;
  • withdraw your consent.

Email vanijyanirmanenterprisespvtltd@gmail.com and we will respond. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
