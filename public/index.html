The difference

Most tools take one slice. We run the pathway.

Record apps and doctor directories solve a piece of the problem and stop at the edge of it. The gap is not features — it is depth.

Record apps & directories

  • Store records or list doctors — one slice of the journey
  • Consumer-side data only, so no supply-side forecasting

  • English and Hindi, built for metros

HealthOS
